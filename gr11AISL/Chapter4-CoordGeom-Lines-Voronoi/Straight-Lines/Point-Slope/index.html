1. Starter Question: Distance & Gradient
Warm-Up

The points A(3, −2) and B(9, 7) lie on a straight line.

  • (a) Calculate the distance between points A and B.
  • (b) Find the gradient of the line passing through A and B.
Reveal Full Starter Solutions
(a) Distance: d = √(9 − 3)2 + (7 − (−2))2 = √62 + 92 = √117 = 3√13 ≈ 10.82
(b) Gradient: m = 7 − (−2)9 − 3 = 96 = 32 (or 1.5)
2. Equation of a Line: Point-Slope Form
Chapter 4
Form 1 • Completed

Gradient-Intercept

y = mx + c

Requires gradient m and the y-intercept c.

Form 2 • Today's Focus

Point-Slope (Point-Gradient)

y − y1 = m(x − x1)

Works with gradient m and any known point (x1, y1)—essential for Voronoi diagrams!

Form 3 • Coming Next

Standard (General) Form

ax + by + d = 0

All coefficients a, b, d written as integers.

Watch the Signs!

Because the formula has minus signs y − y1 = m(x − x1), substituting a negative coordinate flips the sign to a plus!

3. Example 1: Writing Point-Slope Equations
Handout • Example 1

Find the equation of the following lines in point-slope form:

(a) that has a gradient 3 and passes through point A(1, 7)
(b) that passes through the points B(1, 3) and C(2, 5)
Reveal Solutions: Example 1 (a) & (b)
(a) Substitute m = 3 and A(1, 7):
    y − 7 = 3(x − 1)  (Red line: y = 3x + 4)
(b) Find gradient first: m = 5 − 32 − 1 = 2
    y − 3 = 2(x − 1) or y − 5 = 2(x − 2)  (Blue line: y = 2x + 1)
4. Example 2: Boundary Line Application
Handout • Example 2

Points A and B lie on the straight-line boundary between two plots of land as shown in the diagram.

  • (a) Write the equation of the line passing through A and B in point-slope form.
  • (b) A tree is located at point C(25, 30). Determine whether the tree is on the boundary line.
Reveal Full Solution: Example 2
(a) Read coordinates from graph: A(6, 8) and B(12, 10).
    Gradient: m = 10 − 812 − 6 = 26 = 13 ⇒ y − 8 = 13(x − 6) (or y − 10 = 13(x − 12))
(b) Substitute C(25, 30): LHS = 30 − 8 = 22; RHS = 13(25 − 6) = 193 ≈ 6.33.
    Since 22 ≠ 6.33, the tree is NOT on the boundary line.
5. Example 3: Coastal Drone Flight Path
Handout • Example 3

A drone surveys a coastline along a straight flight path, passing over a lighthouse at P(4, 11) and a buoy at Q(10, 8) (coordinates in km).

  • (a) Find the equation of the drone’s flight path in point-slope form.
  • (b) A rescue boat is anchored at R(18, 4), and a second drone flies along y = 2x − 9. Determine whether the rescue boat is on the first drone’s path, and find where the two flight paths intersect.
Reveal Full Solution: Example 3
(a) Gradient: m = 8 − 1110 − 4 = −36 = −12 ⇒ y − 11 = −12(x − 4) (or y = −0.5x + 13)
(b) Test R(18, 4): 4 − 11 = −7 and −12(18 − 4) = −7 ⇒ Yes, R lies on the path.
    Intersection: −0.5x + 13 = 2x − 9 ⇒ 2.5x = 22 ⇒ x = 8.8, y = 2(8.8) − 9 = 8.6 ⇒ (8.8, 8.6).

7. Practice Set: Question 3
Handout • Level 3
Question 3 • Two Points to Point-Slope Form

A straight line passes through the points A(−2, 7) and B(4, −5).

  • (a) Calculate the gradient of the line AB.
  • (b) Write the equation of the line in point-slope form using point A.
Strategy & Answer Check
Step 1: Use m = y2 − y1x2 − x1 carefully with negative signs.
Step 2: Substitute m and A(−2, 7) into y − y1 = m(x − x1).
Reveal Full Solution: Question 3
(a) Gradient: m = −5 − 74 − (−2) = −126 = −2
(b) Using A(−2, 7): y − 7 = −2(x − (−2)) ⇒ y − 7 = −2(x + 2)
8. Practice Set: Question 4
Handout • Level 4
Question 4 • Mountain Gondola Cable

A mountain gondola ascends along a straight cable passing through Base Station M(15, 120) and Mid-Station N(55, 200), where x is horizontal distance (m) and y is elevation (m).

  • (a) Write the equation of the cable in point-slope form (using M).
  • (b) A support pylon is planned at P(95, 275). Determine algebraically whether P lies directly on the cable line.
Strategy & Answer Check
Part (a): Find gradient m between M(15, 120) and N(55, 200), then substitute M.
Part (b): Plug x = 95 and y = 275 into your equation and compare LHS with RHS.
Reveal Full Solution: Question 4
(a) Gradient: m = 200 − 12055 − 15 = 8040 = 2 ⇒ y − 120 = 2(x − 15)
(b) Substitute P(95, 275): LHS = 275 − 120 = 155; RHS = 2(95 − 15) = 2(80) = 160 (cable height at x = 95 is 280 m).
    Since 155 ≠ 160, pylon P does NOT lie on the cable line.
9. Practice Set: Question 5 (IB Challenge)
Handout • Voronoi Preview
Question 5 • Cell Towers & Voronoi Boundary Edge

Two cell towers are located at S(2, 1) and T(8, 5). In a Voronoi diagram, the boundary separating their regions is the perpendicular bisector of segment ST.

  • (a) Find the midpoint M of segment ST and the gradient of ST.
  • (b) Hence, write the equation of the Voronoi boundary edge in point-slope form.
Reveal Full Solution (Question 5)
(a) Midpoint M = (2 + 82, 1 + 52) = (5, 3); Gradient mST = 5 − 18 − 2 = 46 = 23.
(b) Perpendicular gradient: m⊥ = −32 ⇒ y − 3 = −32(x − 5).
