1. The Three Forms of a Straight Line
Unit Roadmap

Just like a number can be written as a fraction, decimal, or percentage, the exact same straight line can be written in three algebraic forms:

Form 1 • Today's Focus

Gradient-Intercept Form

y = mx + c

Best when you know the gradient (m) and the y-intercept (c), or when graphing on your Casio fx-CG50.

Example: y = 2x − 3
Form 2 • Coming Next

Point-Slope Form

y − y1 = m(x − x1)

Best when you know the gradient (m) and any point (x1, y1) on the line.

Example: y − 5 = 2(x − 4)
Form 3 • Final Form

Standard (General) Form

ax + by + d = 0

Required IB exam format where a, b, d are integers (no fractions or decimals).

Example: 2x − y − 3 = 0
Key Takeaway: Notice that gradient (m) is the engine inside both Form 1 and Form 2. Always find m first!

4. Worked Examples: Finding y = mx + c
Teacher Walkthrough
Example 1 • From a Graph

Find the equation of the line shown on the graph in gradient-intercept form.

Reveal Solution (Example 1)
1. Read y-intercept from graph: c = 4 at (0, 4).
2. Find gradient using (0, 4) and (4, 1):
   m = 1 − 44 − 0 = −34
3. Substitute into y = mx + c: y = −34x + 4
Example 2 • From Two Points

Find the equation of the straight line passing through P(2, 5) and Q(6, 13) in the form y = mx + c.

Three-Step Method:
  1. Calculate gradient m.
  2. Substitute m and one point (x, y) into y = mx + c to solve for c.
  3. Write out the final equation.
Reveal Solution (Example 2)
1. m = 13 − 56 − 2 = 84 = 2
2. Substitute m = 2 and P(2, 5) into y = mx + c:
   5 = 2(2) + c ⇒ 5 = 4 + c ⇒ c = 1
3. Final equation: y = 2x + 1

6. Practice Set B: Writing Equations & Modelling
IB AI SL Practice
Question 3 • Two Points

Find the equation of the line passing through (−2, 1) and (4, 10) in the form y = mx + c.

Reveal Full Working (Q3)
m = 10 − 14 − (−2) = 96 = 1.5 (or 32)
10 = 1.5(4) + c ⇒ 10 = 6 + c ⇒ c = 4
Equation: y = 1.5x + 4
Question 4 • IB AI SL Real-World Context

A plumber charges a fixed call-out fee plus an hourly rate. A 2-hour job costs $145, and a 5-hour job costs $280.

Write a linear model C = mt + c for total cost C after t hours:

Reveal Full Working & Interpretation (Q4)
Points: (2, 145) and (5, 280)
m = 280 − 1455 − 2 = 1353 = $45/hour
145 = 45(2) + c ⇒ c = $55 (fixed call-out fee)
Model: C = 45t + 55
