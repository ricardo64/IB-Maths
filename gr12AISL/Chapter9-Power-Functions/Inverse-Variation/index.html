Starter: Fenced Rectangular Garden

A rectangular garden is to be fenced against a long straight brick wall. The garden is formed by using 40 m of fencing for the three remaining sides, as shown in the diagram. Let the length of the two sides perpendicular to the wall be x m.

(a) Show that the area, A, of the garden can be expressed as:

A = 40x − 2x2

(b) Find the dimensions of the garden (the values of the two different side lengths) that will result in the maximum possible area.

Total Fencing = 40 m (3 sides)

Solution: Starter (Fenced Garden)

(a) Expressing area A in terms of x:

Total fencing available for the three sides is 40 m. Since the two sides perpendicular to the wall each have length x m, the remaining side parallel to the brick wall has length (40 − 2x) m.

A = x(40 − 2x) = 40x − 2x2
(b) Dimensions for Maximum Area:

Using the axis of symmetry (or GDC Maximum / dA/dx = 40 − 4x = 0):

x = −b2a = −402(−2) = 10 m

The side parallel to the wall is 40 − 2(10) = 20 m.

Dimensions: 10 m × 20 m (Maximum Area = 200 m2)

Domain: 0 < x < 20

Chapter 9 – Inverse Variation

When one quantity increases at the same rate that another decreases, the two variables are inversely proportional (or vary inversely).

The symbol for variation is ∝ and it stands for “= k × …” (that is, k multiplied by the expression which follows).

For inverse variation:    y ∝ 1xn    or    y = kxn

Note: Multiplying both sides by xn gives the constant product y · xn = k, which is useful for checking values quickly.

Inverse Variation – Worked Example Page 449

Example 15

The number of hours N taken to build a wall varies inversely with the number of people x who are available to work on it.

a When three people are available the wall takes two hours to build. Find the time it takes to build the wall when four people are available to work on it.

b Given it takes three hours to build the wall, state how many people worked on it.

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Time taken when 4 people are available:

Since N varies inversely with x, we write N ∝ 1x, so N = kx.

Substituting x = 3 and N = 2:

2 = k3  ⇒  k = 6  ⇒  N = 6x

When x = 4:

N = 64 = 1.5 hours (1 hour 30 minutes)
(b) Number of people when N = 3 hours:
3 = 6x  ⇒  x = 63 = 2 people

Question 1 — Sound Intensity & Distance

The sound intensity, I (in W m−2), measured from an outdoor concert speaker is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, d metres, from the speaker. At a distance of 4 metres, the sound intensity is 0.75 W m−2.

a Find an equation for I in terms of d. [2]

b Calculate the sound intensity at a distance of 10 metres from the speaker. [1]

c Safety regulations state that the sound intensity must not exceed 0.12 W m−2. Find the minimum distance from the speaker where audience members can safely stand. [2]

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Equation for I in terms of d:

I ∝ 1d2 ⇒ I = kd2. Substituting I = 0.75 and d = 4:

0.75 = k42 = k16  ⇒  k = 12  ⇒  I = 12d2
(b) Sound intensity at d = 10 m:
I = 12102 = 12100 = 0.12 W m−2
(c) Minimum safe distance when I = 0.12 W m−2:
0.12 = 12d2  ⇒  d2 = 120.12 = 100  ⇒  d = 10 m (since d > 0)

Question 2 — Boyle’s Law: Gas Pressure & Volume

At a constant temperature, the pressure P (in kPa) of a fixed mass of gas in a syringe varies inversely with its volume V (in cm3). When the volume of the gas is 45 cm3, the pressure is 120 kPa.

a Write down an equation expressing P in terms of V. [2]

b Find the pressure when the gas is compressed to a volume of 30 cm3. [1]

c Determine the volume of the gas when the pressure drops to 75 kPa. [2]

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Equation for P in terms of V:
P = kV  ⇒  120 = k45  ⇒  k = 5400  ⇒  P = 5400V
(b) Pressure when V = 30 cm3:
P = 540030 = 180 kPa
(c) Volume when P = 75 kPa:
75 = 5400V  ⇒  V = 540075 = 72 cm3

Question 3 — Gravitational Field Strength

The gravitational field strength, g (in N kg−1), of a planet is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, r (in km), from the centre of the planet. On the surface of the planet, where r = 6400 km, the gravitational field strength is 9.8 N kg−1.

a Find the constant of proportionality k, giving your answer in standard form a × 10n correct to 3 significant figures. [2]

b Calculate the gravitational field strength experienced by a satellite orbiting at a distance of 8000 km from the centre of the planet. [3]

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Constant of proportionality k:
g = kr2  ⇒  k = 9.8 × 64002 = 401 408 000 = 4.01 × 108 (3 s.f.)
(b) Gravitational field strength at r = 8000 km:

Using the full unrounded value of k:

g = 401 408 00080002 = 6.272 ≈ 6.27 N kg−1 (3 s.f.)

Question 4 — Illumination & Lamp Distance

The brightness B (in lux) on a desk surface varies inversely with the square of the height h (in metres) of a lamp above the desk. When the lamp is 1.5 m above the desk, the brightness is 320 lux.

a Find the brightness on the desk when the lamp is raised to a height of 2.4 m. [3]

b Find the height to which the lamp must be lowered to achieve a brightness of 500 lux. [2]

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Brightness when h = 2.4 m:

B = kh2 ⇒ k = 320 × 1.52 = 320 × 2.25 = 720, so B = 720h2.

B = 7202.42 = 7205.76 = 125 lux
(b) Height required for B = 500 lux:
500 = 720h2  ⇒  h2 = 720500 = 1.44  ⇒  h = √1.44 = 1.2 m

Question 5 — Guitar String Vibration Frequency

For a guitar string under constant tension, the frequency of vibration, f (in Hz), is inversely proportional to the vibrating length of the string, L (in cm). A string of length 65 cm vibrates at a frequency of 220 Hz.

a Find the frequency when a musician presses the string so that its vibrating length is 50 cm. [3]

b Calculate the percentage increase in frequency when the vibrating length is reduced by 20%. [3]

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Frequency when L = 50 cm:

f = kL ⇒ k = 220 × 65 = 14 300, so f = 14 300L.

f = 14 30050 = 286 Hz
(b) Percentage increase in frequency when L is reduced by 20%:

Reducing L by 20% gives Lnew = 0.8L:

fnew = k0.8L = 10.8 · kL = 1.25f

Since 1.25 = 1 + 0.25, the frequency increases by 25%.
