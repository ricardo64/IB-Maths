Question 1

A juice manufacturer is designing a new open-topped cylindrical glass to hold a volume of 500 cm3. The glass has a radius of r cm and a height of h cm.

(a) Show that the total surface area, A, of the glass is given by:

A = πr2 + 1000r

(b) Using your GDC make a sketch of function A for the domain 0 ≤ r ≤ 10.

(c) Calculate the value of r that minimizes the surface area of the glass.

Full worked solution is on Slide 2

Starter – Worked Solution Worked Solution

Solution: Question 1 (Cylindrical Glass)

(a) Expressing A in terms of r:

Volume of cylinder: V = πr2h = 500, so h = 500πr2.

Total surface area (one circular base plus the curved side):

A = πr2 + 2πrh = πr2 + 2πr500πr2 = πr2 + 1000r
(b) GDC Sketch for 0 ≤ r ≤ 10:

There is a vertical asymptote at r = 0. The curve decreases steeply to a local minimum and then rises smoothly as r increases toward 10.

(c) Value of r that minimizes A:

Using GDC Minimum (or setting dA/dr = 0):

2πr − 1000r2 = 0  ⇒  r3 = 500π  ⇒  r = 3√500π ≈ 5.42 cm (3 s.f.)
GDC Window: r ∈ [0, 10], A ∈ [0, 600]

Chapter 9 – Direct Variation

In Chapter 5, Linear functions, we looked at Direct Variation. We will start by looking further into this.

The symbol for variation is ∝ and it stands for “= k × …” (that is, k multiplied by the variable which follows).

For direct variation:    y ∝ xn    or    y = kxn

Variation provides an alternative to the use of functions when representing real-life situations.

Direct Variation – Worked Example Example 1 of 2

Example 1

The distance, d metres, that a rock falls varies directly with the square of the time taken, t seconds.

a If the rock falls 6 metres in 2 seconds, write an equation for d in terms of t.

b Find the distance the rock has fallen after 5 seconds.

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Equation for d in terms of t:

Since d varies directly with the square of t, we write d ∝ t2, so d = kt2.

Substituting d = 6 and t = 2:

6 = k(2)2  ⇒  6 = 4k  ⇒  k = 64 = 1.5

Equation:   d = 1.5t2

(b) Distance fallen after 5 seconds:

Substituting t = 5 into d = 1.5t2:

d = 1.5(5)2 = 1.5 × 25 = 37.5 metres
Direct Variation – Worked Example Example 2 of 2

Example 2

The volume V of a cube (shape) is directly proportional to the cube (power) of its side length x.

(a) Write an equation for V in terms of x, and identify the constant of proportionality k, given that when x = 4 the volume is 512 cm3.

(b) Find the side length of a cube whose volume is 1000 cm3.

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
(a) Equation and constant of proportionality k:

Since V ∝ x3, we have V = kx3. Substituting V = 512 and x = 4:

512 = k(4)3  ⇒  512 = 64k  ⇒  k = 51264 = 8

Equation:   V = 8x3   (Constant of proportionality k = 8)

(b) Side length when V = 1000 cm3:
1000 = 8x3  ⇒  x3 = 10008 = 125  ⇒  x = 3√125 = 5
Challenge Problem Extension

EXTENSION!

Continuing from Example 2 (V = kx3):

(c) If the side length of the cube is increased by 20%, by what percentage does the volume increase?

Working Pad (Collapses on Reveal Solution)
Percentage Increase in Volume:

Increasing side length x by 20% multiplies x by a scale factor of 1.2, so xnew = 1.2x.

Vnew = k(1.2x)3 = 1.23 · kx3 = 1.728V

Since 1.728 = 1 + 0.728, the volume increases by 72.8%.
