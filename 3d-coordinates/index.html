The 2D Midpoint Formula

In a 2D space, the midpoint M joining A(x1, y1) and B(x2, y2) is:

M = (
x1 + x22
,
y1 + y22
)

The Midpoint Formula in 3D (Page 152)
Definition

In a three-dimensional (3D) space, the midpoint, M, of the line segment joining points A(x1, y1, z1) and B(x2, y2, z2) is:

M = (
x1 + x22
,
y1 + y22
,
z1 + z22
)
Key Takeaway

Just like in 2D, finding the midpoint in 3D is simply calculating the average of the x-coordinates, y-coordinates, and z-coordinates.
